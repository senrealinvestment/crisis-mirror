Crisis Mirror Architecture

NO WEARABLE CAMERA · v1

Glasses = mics + discreet HUD (+ bone conduction when available).   Phone / iPad = real-time brain.   OpenEvidence / UpToDate = optional deep consult.   No face-worn video.

One sentence: Glasses listen (no camera) and show a short cue · phone/iPad decides the whisper from your local protocols · OpenEvidence/UpToDate only on demand · after the case you grade the cue · learning stays offline.

Glasses (thin · no cam)

  • • Mics for ambient audio
  • • Discrete HUD text
  • • Tone: BC on Halo, or phone/buds on G2
  • • No wearable camera

Phone / iPad (brain)

  • • Gate → detect → draft cue
  • • Local Tx ICU protocol pack
  • • Safety rules (no auto-orders)
  • • Label taps + audio-out fallback

Cloud (consultant)

  • • OpenEvidence / UpToDate-class
  • • Only after trigger or on demand
  • • Stripped questions, not room audio
  • • Private cloud room when needed

Learn (later)

  • • Helpful? Accurate?
  • • Latency and cost per cue
  • • Offline update only
  • • Sim lab first

Hardware paths

  • • G2: clinical / no-cam native
  • • Halo: open + BC; cam hard-off
  • • Phone-only valid before glasses
  • • No perfect open+BC+no-cam device yet

Why drop the camera

  • • PHI / consent / unit culture
  • • Easier compliance story
  • • Speech + protocols carry v1 value
  • • Vision = separate future program
